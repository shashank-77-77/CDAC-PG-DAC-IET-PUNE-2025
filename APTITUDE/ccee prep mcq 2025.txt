## Percentages and ratios

- **Percent to fraction:**
  
  \[
  25\%=\frac{1}{4},\quad 12.5\%=\frac{1}{8},\quad 33\frac{1}{3}\%=\frac{1}{3}
  \]

- **Increase/decrease:**
  
  \[
  \text{New value}= \text{Original}\times\left(1\pm\frac{\text{percent}}{100}\right)
  \]

- **Successive changes (don’t add):**
  
  \[
  \text{Net factor}= \left(1+\frac{a}{100}\right)\left(1+\frac{b}{100}\right)
  \]

- **Ratio to parts:**
  
  \[
  \text{If ratio }a:b,\ \text{total }T\Rightarrow \text{parts }=a+b,\ \text{values }=\frac{a}{a+b}T,\ \frac{b}{a+b}T
  \]

---

## Averages and mixtures

- **Average:**
  
  \[
  \text{Average}=\frac{\text{Sum of terms}}{\text{Number of terms}}
  \]

- **Adding one item:**
  
  \[
  \text{New average}=\frac{n\cdot A + x}{n+1}
  \]

- **Weighted average (mixtures):**
  
  \[
  A_{\text{mix}}=\frac{w_1A_1+w_2A_2}{w_1+w_2}
  \]

- **Alligation shortcut (two components):**
  
  \[
  \frac{\text{Quantity of cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of dearer}}=\frac{A_d-A_m}{A_m-A_c}
  \]
  
  where \(A_c\) is cheaper value, \(A_d\) dearer value, \(A_m\) mean value.

## Time, speed, and distance

- **Core relation:**
  
  \[
  \text{Distance}= \text{Speed}\times \text{Time}
  \]

- **Relative speed (same direction vs opposite):**
  
  \[
  v_{\text{rel}}=
  \begin{cases}
  |v_1-v_2| & \text{same direction}\\
  v_1+v_2 & \text{opposite direction}
  \end{cases}
  \]

- **Average speed (two equal distances):**
  
  \[
  v_{\text{avg}}=\frac{2v_1v_2}{v_1+v_2}
  \]

- **Trains crossing:**
  
  \[
  t=\frac{\text{sum of lengths (or length vs point)}}{\text{relative speed}}
  \]

---

## Work and pipes

- **Work rate:**
  
  \[
  \text{Rate}=\frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Time}},\quad \text{Total rate}=\sum \text{individual rates}
  \]

- **Combined time:**
  
  \[
  T_{\text{combined}}=\frac{1}{\sum \frac{1}{T_i}}
  \]

- **Pipes (inlet/outlet):**
  
  \[
  \text{Net rate}=\text{inlet rates}-\text{outlet rates}
  \]

---

## Profit, loss, and simple interest

- **Profit/loss percent:**
  
  \[
  \%\ =\frac{\text{Profit or Loss}}{\text{Cost Price}}\times 100
  \]

- **Marked price/discount:**
  
  \[
  \text{Selling Price}= \text{Marked Price}\times\left(1-\frac{d}{100}\right)
  \]

- **Simple interest:**
  
  \[
  \text{SI}= \frac{P\cdot R\cdot T}{100},\quad \text{Amount}=P+\text{SI}
  \]

---

## Permutations, combinations, and probability

- **Permutations:**
  
  \[
  {}^nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}
  \]

- **Combinations:**
  
  \[
  {}^nC_r=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}
  \]

- **Basic probability:**
  
  \[
  P(\text{event})=\frac{\text{favorable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}}
  \]

- **Independent events:**
  
  \[
  P(A\cap B)=P(A)\cdot P(B)
  \]

---

## Number systems and divisibility

- **LCM/GCD relation:**
  
  \[
  \text{LCM}\cdot \text{GCD}=a\cdot b
  \]

- **Divisibility quick checks:**
  - **By 3:** Sum of digits divisible by 3.
  - **By 4:** Last two digits form a number divisible by 4.
  - **By 8:** Last three digits divisible by 8.
  - **By 11:** Alternating sum of digits divisible by 11.
